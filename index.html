06 / NEW WALLETREADY
Make a backup card now — without one, losing this card loses the wallet.

A wallet is one secret seed in one file. Every address and key is derived from it, so the file plus its passphrase is the whole wallet.

New wallets are saved to ~/.xcoin/ and appear in the WALLET tab’s file list.

Normal wallet: the seed is shown once, right after creation. Write it on paper; it is the only backup.

Card-bound wallet: the seed is sealed to an NTAG 424 card you tap during creation and is never shown. Have a blank card ready on the NFC reader. Your backup is a second card: MAKE BACKUP CARD (here right after creation, or in the WALLET tab) copies it to a blank card.
